Ordering

How to order

Order on this website or on WhatsApp, then pick up or get it delivered.

  1. Build your order

    Add drinks and extras, then choose pickup or delivery and a time.

  2. Send it on WhatsApp

    Tap send and WhatsApp opens with your order typed out. We'll reply to confirm.

  3. Pay by cash or DuitNow QR

    Pay cash when you collect or when we deliver, or scan our DuitNow QR and send us the receipt.

Last orders at 8:30pm. We're open every day from 11am to 9pm.
