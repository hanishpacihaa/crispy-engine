      Five friends, one coffee machine.

      CMC started with five friends and one problem: good coffee was either too far from campus or too expensive for a student budget. So we decided to make it ourselves.

      We practised shot after shot between classes until every cup tasted like something we'd happily pay more for. Then we kept the price student-friendly.

      Today we brew specialty coffee from our home-based café at Mahallah Zaid bin Harithah. Every drink is made to order, and every cup should feel like a small break in a long day. That's what Coffee Make Cozy means to us.
