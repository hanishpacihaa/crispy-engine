Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Coffee Make Cozy (CMC) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website and order from us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and delivery address, when you send us an order or feedback through WhatsApp or email.
  • The details of your order, such as drinks, add-ons, pickup or delivery time and payment method.

How we use it

  • To prepare, confirm and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order or reply to your feedback.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Information saved on your device

This website saves your cart, language choice and contact details in your own browser, so you don't have to type them again. Nothing is sent to us until you choose to send an order or feedback. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Payments

We don't collect card or bank details on this website. Cash is paid in person, and DuitNow QR payments are made through your own banking or e-wallet app.

Other services

Orders and feedback are sent through WhatsApp or email, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

In line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Contact us at coffeemakecozy@gmail.com or +60 11-2918 6169.
